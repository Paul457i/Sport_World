Alica Schmidt

Personal information:

  • Full Name: Alica Megan Schmidt
  • Country: Germany
  • Date of Birth: 8 November 1998
  • Sport: Athletics (Track and Field)
  • Speciality: 400-metre and 800-metre running
  • Main Events: Individual track races and the 4 × 400-metre relay
  • Club: SCC Berlin

Description

Alica Schmidt is a German track-and-field athlete known for her speed and participation in international athletics competitions. She initially specialised in the 400-metre sprint and the 4 × 400-metre relay before expanding her focus to the 800-metre event. In athletics, the 400 metres involves running one complete lap of a standard outdoor track, while the 800 metres involves running two laps. In the 4 × 400-metre relay, four athletes each run 400 metres, passing a baton from one runner to the next. Role in Her Sport Alica competes as a middle-distance and sprint runner, depending on the event. In relay races, she represents Germany by running one of the four legs and helping her team achieve the fastest possible overall time. Her sport requires speed, endurance, disciplined training, and effective baton exchanges between teammates.

Major Achievements
  1. 2017 European Athletics U20 Championships: Won a silver medal as part of Germany’s 4 × 400-metre relay team.
  2. 2019 European Athletics U23 Championships: Won a bronze medal in the 4 × 400-metre relay.
  3. 2023 World Athletics Championships: Finished seventh in the women’s 4 × 400-metre relay with the German team.
  4. 2024 European Athletics Championships: Finished eighth in the women’s 4 × 400-metre relay.
  5. 2024 Paris Olympic Games: Represented Germany in athletics.

These accomplishments demonstrate her experience competing at European, world, and Olympic levels. Official athlete profile: World Athletics — Alica Schmidt⁠

Christiano Ronaldo

Personal Information
  • Full Name: Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro
  • Country: Portugal
  • Date of Birth: 5 February 1985
  • Sport: Football (Soccer)
  • Position: Forward
  • Current Club: Al-Nassr, Saudi Arabia
  • National Team: Portugal
Description

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese professional footballer widely recognised for his goal-scoring ability, athleticism, and achievements in international and club football.

He has played for several major European clubs, including Manchester United, Real Madrid, and Juventus. He currently plays for Al-Nassr in the Saudi Pro League and represents Portugal internationally. His career has included numerous domestic league titles, European trophies, and individual awards.

Ronaldo is particularly known for his powerful shots, accurate finishing, heading ability, speed, and movement inside the penalty area.

Role in His Sport

Ronaldo plays as a forward, primarily focusing on scoring goals and creating attacking opportunities.

His main responsibilities include:
  • Scoring goals with his feet and head.
  • Finding space in the opposition’s penalty area.
  • Making attacking runs to receive passes.
  • Supporting teammates during attacking moves.
  • Taking penalties and contributing to set-piece situations.

His combination of positioning, finishing, and aerial ability has made him a prominent goal scorer throughout his career.

Major Achievements
  1. Five Ballon d’Or awards: Recognised as one of the world’s leading footballers on five occasions.
  2. UEFA European Championship 2016: Captained Portugal to its first European Championship title.
  3. UEFA Nations League: Helped Portugal win the competition in 2019 and 2025.
  4. UEFA Champions League: Won the competition five times during his career.
  5. Real Madrid’s all-time leading goal scorer: Scored 451 goals in 438 competitive appearances for the Spanish club.
  6. Saudi Pro League Golden Boot, 2023–24: Scored 35 goals in 31 league appearances, setting a new single-season scoring record for the competition at that time.

Ronaldo’s career is notable for his longevity, goal-scoring records, and contributions to Portugal’s international success.

Lamine Yamal

Personal Information
  • Full Name: Lamine Yamal Nasraoui Ebana
  • Country: Spain
  • Date of Birth: 13 July 2007
  • Sport: Football (Soccer)
  • Position: Right winger / Forward
  • Club: FC Barcelona
  • National Team: Spain
Description

Lamine Yamal is a Spanish professional footballer who plays for FC Barcelona and the Spanish national team. He became widely known for his technical ability, close ball control, dribbling, and creativity despite his young age.

Yamal developed through Barcelona’s famous youth academy, La Masia, before progressing to the first team. He has established himself as an attacking player capable of creating scoring opportunities and contributing goals.

His playing style combines quick changes of direction, accurate passing, attacking movement, and the ability to take on defenders in one-on-one situations.

Role in His Sport

Yamal primarily plays as a right winger, an attacking position on the right side of the pitch.

His main responsibilities include:
  • Dribbling past opposing defenders.
  • Creating goal-scoring chances for teammates.
  • Delivering crosses and dangerous passes into the penalty area.
  • Cutting inside to shoot with his left foot.
  • Scoring goals and supporting the team’s attacking play.

His creativity and ability to operate in tight spaces make him an important attacking option for Barcelona and Spain.

Major Achievements
  1. UEFA European Championship 2024: Helped Spain win the tournament in Germany.
  2. Youngest player in European Championship history: Made his tournament debut against Croatia at 16 years and 338 days old.
  3. Youngest goalscorer in European Championship history: Scored against France in the Euro 2024 semi-final.
  4. Kopa Trophy 2024: Won the award recognising the best under-21 player in world football.
  5. FC Barcelona’s 2024–25 domestic treble: Helped Barcelona win La Liga, the Copa del Rey, and the Spanish Super Cup.
  6. La Liga Player of the Season, 2025–26: Received the award after an outstanding season with Barcelona.

These achievements highlight his rapid development and his contributions to both club and international football.
